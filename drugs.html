Drugs

Drugs and prevention

No drug is approved to prevent Alzheimer’s disease. These are the medicines being tested before symptoms, those that failed, and existing medicines with prevention signals.

Existing medicines with prevention signals

DrugTypeFor prevention
Blood-pressure medicinesGenericSee evidenceResults ↗Lower blood pressureIntensive treatment reduced mild cognitive impairment in SPRINT MIND; not approved for dementia prevention.
Cholesterol-lowering medicinesGenericSee risk factorsLancet Commission ↗Lower LDL cholesterolHigh midlife LDL cholesterol is linked to about 7% of dementia; trial evidence for prevention is still limited.
Shingles vaccineOlder live vaccine (Zostavax)See evidenceStudy ↗Vaccine against herpes zosterAbout 20% fewer new dementia diagnoses in a Wales natural experiment; not a randomized trial.
