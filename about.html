About this site

What this resource is, who maintains it, and how information is chosen.

This site is maintained by Mahdi Moqri, PhD, in a personal capacity. It is not an official resource of any employer or institution, and it is not medical advice.

Every figure links to its source, preferring peer-reviewed papers, trial registries and official reports. Trial results are shown as reported, and observational findings are labelled as such.

It is a sister site to apoe4.science, which focuses on people with two copies of APOE4.
