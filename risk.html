Risk factors

Fourteen risk factors you can change

The 2024 Lancet Commission estimated that about 45% of dementia worldwide is linked to 14 modifiable risk factors across the life course.

Life stageRisk factor
Early lifeLess education
MidlifeHearing loss
MidlifeHigh LDL cholesterol
MidlifeDepression
MidlifeTraumatic brain injury
MidlifePhysical inactivity
MidlifeDiabetes
MidlifeSmoking
MidlifeHigh blood pressure
MidlifeObesity
MidlifeExcessive alcohol
Later lifeSocial isolation
Later lifeAir pollution
Later lifeUntreated vision loss

Two factors were new in 2024: high LDL cholesterol in midlife, estimated to account for about 7% of dementia, and untreated vision loss in later life, about 2%. ↗

The 45% estimate assumes each factor is causal and could be removed entirely, so it shows the size of the opportunity rather than a promise for any one person.

Genes matter too. Two copies of APOE4 raise risk far more than any single factor here; see the sister site apoe4.science.

This site is for education and is not medical advice. Talk with your doctor about your own risk and what is right for you.
