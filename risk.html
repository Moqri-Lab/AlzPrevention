Risk factors

Fourteen risk factors you can change

The 2024 Lancet Commission estimated that about 45% of dementia worldwide is linked to 14 modifiable risk factors across the life course.

Life stageRisk factorWhat can help
Early lifeLess educationGood-quality education for all children; keep learning throughout life
MidlifeHearing lossGet hearing tested; use hearing aids if needed
MidlifeHigh LDL cholesterolCheck cholesterol from about age 40 and treat if high
MidlifeDepressionSeek treatment for depression
MidlifeTraumatic brain injuryWear helmets and seatbelts; protect against head injury in sport
MidlifePhysical inactivityStay physically active through life
MidlifeDiabetesPrevent and manage diabetes
MidlifeSmokingStop smoking, at any age
MidlifeHigh blood pressureCheck blood pressure and treat if high
MidlifeObesityMaintain a healthy weight
MidlifeExcessive alcoholLimit alcohol
Later lifeSocial isolationStay socially connected
Later lifeAir pollutionReduce exposure to air pollution where possible
Later lifeUntreated vision lossGet eyes checked; treat vision problems such as cataracts

Two factors were new in 2024: high LDL cholesterol in midlife, estimated to account for about 7% of dementia, and untreated vision loss in later life, about 2%. ↗

The suggested actions follow the Commission’s recommendations; they are general advice, not a personal plan. ↗

The 45% estimate assumes each factor is causal and could be removed entirely, so it shows the size of the opportunity rather than a promise for any one person.

Genes matter too. Two copies of APOE4 raise risk far more than any single factor here; see the sister site apoe4.science.

This site is for education and is not medical advice. Talk with your doctor about your own risk and what is right for you.
