Papers

Key papers on prevention

The studies behind this site, grouped by topic.

Risk factors

  • Livingston G, et al. Dementia prevention, intervention, and care: 2024 report of the Lancet standing Commission. Lancet 2024.

    The 14 modifiable risk factors and the 45% estimate.

Lifestyle and medical trials

  • Ngandu T, et al. A 2 year multidomain intervention of diet, exercise, cognitive training, and vascular risk monitoring versus control to prevent cognitive decline in at-risk elderly people (FINGER): a randomised controlled trial. Lancet 2015.

    First large trial to show a multidomain lifestyle program helps cognition.

  • SPRINT MIND Investigators (Williamson JD, et al.). Effect of intensive vs standard blood pressure control on probable dementia. JAMA 2019.

    Intensive blood pressure control reduced mild cognitive impairment.

  • Lin FR, et al. Hearing intervention versus health education control to reduce cognitive decline in older adults with hearing loss in the USA (ACHIEVE). Lancet 2023.

    No overall effect; possible benefit in people at higher risk.

  • U.S. POINTER Study Group. Two-year results of the U.S. POINTER lifestyle trial. Published in JAMA, July 2025.

    Both programs improved cognition; the structured one more.

  • Eyting M, et al. A natural experiment on the effect of herpes zoster vaccination on dementia. Nature 2025.

    Shingles vaccination linked to fewer new dementia diagnoses in Wales.

Drug prevention trials

  • Sperling RA, et al. Trial of solanezumab in preclinical Alzheimer’s disease. New England Journal of Medicine 2023.

    The A4 Study: no slowing of decline.

  • Donanemab in preclinical Alzheimer’s disease: screening and baseline data from TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 3. 2025.

    Who is enrolled in the largest ongoing prevention trial.
